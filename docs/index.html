Dreams Are Also Experiences 梦境也是经历

"Dreams are not illusions. They are lived moments—dense with emotion and sensation." “梦境不是幻觉。它们是鲜活的时刻——充满了情感和感觉。”

Deep Emotion 深度情感

Dreams activate emotion-related brain regions and can feel as real as waking life. "Dreams feel so vivid—like reliving another life. They have a real emotional impact." 梦境激活大脑中与情感相关的区域，感觉与清醒生活一样真实。“梦境感觉如此生动——就像重活了一次。它们具有真实的情感冲击力。”

Rapid Forgetting 快速遗忘

We dream 4–6 times per night, but most details disappear within 5–10 minutes of waking. Recording dreams is archiving a psychological state before it disappears. 我们每晚做梦 4-6 次，但大多数细节在醒来后 5-10 分钟内消失。记录梦境是在心理状态消失前将其存档。

Research & Case Studies 研究与案例

Nature Neuroscience (2023)

Found that dream emotion activates the same brain regions as real experience—proving the biological basis of emotional dreaming. Dreams are therefore not "less real"—they are another mode of emotional reality. 研究发现，梦境情感激活的大脑区域与真实经历相同——证明了情感梦境的生物学基础。因此，梦境并非“不真实”，而是情感现实的另一种模式。

MIT Media Lab — Dormio

A wearable system that shapes targeted dreams through auditory cues, enhancing creativity and emotional regulation. 一种可穿戴系统，通过听觉线索塑造目标梦境，增强创造力和情绪调节能力。

Why We Archive Dreams 为什么我们要存档梦境

Emotional Archive 情感档案

Writing or recording a dream turns a vanishing feeling into a traceable emotional state in your personal timeline. It prevents the slow erosion of memory and feeling. 书写或记录梦境将消逝的感觉转化为个人时间轴上可追溯的情感状态。它防止了记忆和感觉的缓慢侵蚀。

Shared Understanding 共识与理解

Sharing dreams turns private images into shared language. Jung’s concept of the collective unconscious helps explain why certain dream motifs resonate across people. 分享梦境将私密图像转化为共同语言。荣格的集体潜意识概念有助于解释为什么某些梦境主题会在不同人之间产生共鸣。
